Sandemonium: Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Last updated 30 October 2026 · Cairo Kernel

This policy explains what information the Android game Sandemonium (package com.cairokernel.sandemonium, on Google Play) collects, and how it is used. Sandemonium is made by the independent developer Cairo Kernel ("we", "us").

In short: the current version of Sandemonium does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

None. The current version of the game does not send any personal or device information to us or to anyone else. The only way information from you reaches us through the game is an email you choose to send (see section 4).

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so you can continue where you left off: the level you are on, the levels you have completed and simple play counts (wins, fails and undos). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

The Daily Challenge is created on your device from the current date (in UTC). Nothing is downloaded to make it, and nothing about it is sent anywhere.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. This version has no in-game switch for it yet.
  • Network access: the game may declare internet access, but the current version does not use it to send data.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. If you email us

The Send feedback button in the game's about panel opens your own email app with our address and the game's version number filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. If you do, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to reply to you and to improve the game. We do not share or sell them, and we delete them if you ask. The same applies to any email you send us directly.

5. Google Play

You download Sandemonium through Google Play, which is run by Google and covered by Google's Privacy Policy. If you have allowed it on your device, Google may share aggregated, anonymous crash and performance reports with us through the Play Console. We use them only to fix bugs and improve performance. They do not identify you.

6. Children

Sandemonium is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

7. Your rights

Because the game does not collect personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, export or delete, apart from any email you have sent us. If you live in a region with privacy rights (for example the EU, the UK or California) and have a question or request, email us and we will reply.

8. Changes to this policy

Future versions of Sandemonium may add advertising, analytics or crash reporting. Before any version that does so is released, we will update this policy to describe exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and the game will ask for your consent where the law requires it. The date at the top of this page always shows the latest version.
