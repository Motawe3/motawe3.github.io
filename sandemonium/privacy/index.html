Sandemonium: Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Cairo Kernel

This policy explains what information the Android game Sandemonium (package com.cairokernel.sandemonium, on Google Play) collects, and how it is used. Sandemonium is made by the independent developer Cairo Kernel ("we", "us").

In short: the current version of Sandemonium does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

None. The current version of the game does not send any personal or device information to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (for example levels completed, sound and vibration preferences) on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in the game's settings.
  • Network access: the game may declare internet access, but the current version does not use it to send data.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Google Play

You download Sandemonium through Google Play, which is run by Google and covered by Google's Privacy Policy. If you have allowed it on your device, Google may share aggregated, anonymous crash and performance reports with us through the Play Console. We use them only to fix bugs and improve performance. They do not identify you.

5. Children

Sandemonium is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, export or delete. If you live in a region with privacy rights (for example the EU, the UK or California) and have a question or request, email us and we will reply.

7. Changes to this policy

Future versions of Sandemonium may add advertising, analytics or crash reporting. Before any version that does so is released, we will update this policy to describe exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and the game will ask for your consent where the law requires it. The date at the top of this page always shows the latest version.
