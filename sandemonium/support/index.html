Sandemonium: Support

Updated 31 October 2026 · Cairo Kernel

Sandemonium is a sand puzzle for Android: pour coloured sand into matching pits and flush them.

Need help? Email cairokernel@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the game version. The version is at the bottom of the game's Settings (the gear on the map, or Pause → Settings).

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

  1. Touch the strip under the board, or anywhere in the lower part of the screen. The block above that column lights up, and a faint column shows where its sand will fall.
  2. Drag left or right to aim.
  3. Lift your finger to pour. A tap without a drag pours the block where it stands.

Fill every pit with sand of its own colour to win. Each cube of sand fills one square of a pit. Sand of the wrong colour slides off the pit and is lost.

I made a mistake. Can I take it back?

Yes. Undo rewinds your last pour, and Restart starts the level again. Restarting is always free.

Why did I fail the level?

The fail panel tells you why in one sentence, for example "No blocks left: the mint pit still needs 1 cube." or "No moves left: every block is frozen." Undo or Restart to try again.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. There is no account and we keep no copy. If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the game's progress in your Google backup, so it can come back when you reinstall or move to a new phone. Clearing the game's data (Android Settings → Apps → Sandemonium → Storage → Clear storage) erases it from the phone.

When does the Daily Challenge reset?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same puzzle that day. Open it from the calendar button on the map (the map appears after level 5). Winning it pays coins and keeps your daily streak going.

Are there lives or energy?

No. Play as long as you like; there is nothing to wait for. Coins and boosters are earned in the game. This version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does the game work offline?

Yes, completely. It never needs an internet connection, and that includes the Daily Challenge.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer.

How do I join the test?

Sandemonium is in a closed test on Google Play.

  1. Ask us for an invitation by email, then join our testers' Google Group with the Google account you use on your phone.
  2. Open the test link: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.cairokernel.sandemonium, and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Sandemonium from Google Play. It can take a few minutes to appear.
  4. Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. It helps the game reach the public store.

How do I leave the test?

Open the same test link and tap Leave the program. Then uninstall the game. You can also leave the Google Group.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

In the game, open Settings, then Support. Your email app opens with our address and the game version filled in. You can also email cairokernel@gmail.com directly. Screenshots help a lot.

What data does the game collect?

None. See the privacy policy.
